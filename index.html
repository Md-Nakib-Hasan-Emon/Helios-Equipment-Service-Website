Common questions

Quick answers before you get in touch.

How often does a screw compressor need an oil change?

Every 4,000 operating hours or once a year, whichever comes first. Use the oil grade named on the unit nameplate, and depressurise and isolate the unit before draining.

What happens after I raise a service case?

You receive an acknowledgement with your case number. The case is routed to your regional queue, where a dispatcher assigns an engineer.

How do I know whether a visit is chargeable?

It depends on the coverage plan for the unit. We check it before the engineer travels and tell you whether the plan is active, expired or missing.

Can I add details to a case I already raised?

Yes. Reply to the acknowledgement email and your message is added to the same case, so the engineer sees the whole conversation in one place.
